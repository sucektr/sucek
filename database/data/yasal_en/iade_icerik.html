Last Updated: June 2026


1. Return Conditions

You may request a return within 14 days of the date you receive the product. Products to be returned must:

• Be unused and in their original packaging

• Be complete with all accessories and documents

• Be undamaged

These conditions are required.


2. Non-Returnable Products

The following products are not eligible for return:

• Products manufactured or personalized specifically for the customer

• Hygiene products (packaging opened)

• Discounted or promotional products (if specifically stated)

• Damaged products or products with missing parts


3. Return Process

1. Send your return request to info@sucek.com.tr.

2. You will be provided with a return code and instructions.

3. Ship the product in its original packaging with the return code.

4. The product will be inspected within 5 business days after it reaches us.

5. For approved returns, payment is refunded to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.


4. Exchange Conditions

Free exchange is provided in the case of incorrect or defective product shipment. A price difference may be charged for exchange requests involving a different product. Exchanges follow the same steps as the return process.


5. Shipping Fee

Return shipping for defective or incorrect products is covered by SUÇEK. For returns made under the right of withdrawal, the shipping fee is the responsibility of the BUYER.
