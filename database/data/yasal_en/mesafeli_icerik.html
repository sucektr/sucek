ARTICLE 2 — SUBJECT

This agreement sets out the rights and obligations of the parties, pursuant to Law No. 6502 on the Protection of Consumers and the Regulation on Distance Contracts, regarding the sale and delivery of products ordered electronically by the BUYER through SUÇEK's website.


ARTICLE 3 — PRODUCT INFORMATION

The essential characteristics of the product, its price (VAT included), and payment and delivery information are provided on the order page and in the order confirmation email.


ARTICLE 4 — PAYMENT

Payment may be made by credit/debit card or bank transfer/EFT. Credit card payments are processed securely through the PayTR infrastructure. Card information is not transferred to SUÇEK.


ARTICLE 5 — DELIVERY

The order is shipped within 3–7 business days of payment confirmation. Delivery time may vary depending on the courier company and the buyer's location. A special delivery option may be offered for orders within Ankara.


ARTICLE 6 — RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL

The BUYER may withdraw from the agreement within 14 days of the delivery date, without giving any reason and without paying a penalty. Notice of withdrawal must be submitted to the SELLER in writing.

Cases where the right of withdrawal cannot be exercised:

• Products manufactured or personalized specifically for the customer

• Products unsuitable for return due to hygiene reasons (packaging opened)

• Digital content (if download has been initiated)


ARTICLE 7 — RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

The separately published Return & Exchange Policy applies to return and exchange terms.


ARTICLE 8 — DISPUTE RESOLUTION

The Courts and Enforcement Offices of Ankara have jurisdiction over disputes arising from this agreement. Consumers may apply to the Ankara Provincial/District Consumer Arbitration Committee for complaints.


ARTICLE 9 — EFFECTIVE DATE

By completing the order, the BUYER declares that they have read and accepted this agreement. The agreement takes effect upon order confirmation.
