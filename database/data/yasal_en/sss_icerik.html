Orders and Payment

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit card, debit card and bank transfer/EFT. Payments are processed through the PayTR secure payment infrastructure.


How can I track my order?

You can use the tracking number in your order confirmation email, or track your orders from your member panel.


How can I get an invoice?

Your invoice is sent together with the product at the time of delivery. For e-invoice requests, please contact us.


Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Orders are shipped within 3–7 business days of payment confirmation. A special delivery option may be offered for orders within Ankara.


How much is the shipping fee?

Shipping fees vary depending on the order amount and delivery region. Free shipping may apply to orders above a certain amount.


Architecture and Construction Services

Do you provide service outside Ankara?

Yes, we also provide service for projects outside Ankara. Site surveys within Ankara are free of charge.


How can I book a free site survey?

You can book online through our website or call (+90) 312 557 21 41.


How long does the permit process take?

It varies depending on the municipality and the size of the project, but generally takes 30–90 days on average.


Membership and Account

Can I shop without becoming a member?

Yes, you can shop as a guest. However, member login is required for features such as order tracking and favorite products.


I forgot my password, what should I do?

You can click "Forgot Password" on the login page to have a password reset link sent to your email address. You can also email info@sucek.com.tr for help.
