1. Identity of the Data Controller

Under Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), your personal data is processed by SUÇEK, acting as the Data Controller, for the purposes described below.

2. Personal Data Processed

SUÇEK may process the following personal data of yours:

• Name, surname, national ID number

• Contact information: email address, phone number, address

• Payment information (card numbers are never stored)

• User account information and purchase history

• Cookie and site usage data.

3. Purpose of Processing Personal Data

Your personal data is processed for the following purposes:

• Fulfilling contractual obligations

• Carrying out order, delivery and invoicing processes

• Customer service and complaint management

• Fulfilling legal obligations

• Marketing and promotional activities (where consent has been given)

• Site security and fraud prevention

4. Transfer of Personal Data

Your personal data may be shared with cargo and logistics companies, payment institutions, public authorities as required by legal obligations, and domestic business partners.

5. Method of Collecting Personal Data and Its Legal Basis

Your data is collected through the website, mobile application, email, phone and verbal channels. The legal grounds for processing, under Article 5/2 of the KVKK, are the establishment and performance of a contract, legitimate interest, and explicit consent.

6. Rights of the Data Subject

Pursuant to Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the following rights:

• To learn whether your personal data is being processed

• To request information if it has been processed

• To learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose

• To learn the third parties to whom it is transferred domestically or abroad

• To request correction if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly

• To request its deletion or destruction

• To request that correction or deletion be notified to third parties to whom the data has been transferred

• To object to a result that is to your detriment arising from analysis carried out solely through automated systems

• To request compensation for damages arising from unlawful processing

You may submit your requests in writing to info@sucek.com.tr.
